# Unrolled Support

Unrolled turns your camera roll into a year-in-photos recap. Everything runs on your iPhone; your photos are never uploaded.

## Common questions

**Restore my purchase.** Open any locked card, tap **Unlock**, then **Restore purchase**. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

**Some photos are missing from my recap.** If you chose *Limit Access*, Unrolled only sees the photos you selected. Go to Settings → Apps → Unrolled → Photos → Full Access. Photos stored only in iCloud may be skipped.

**Captions look generic.** Personalized captions need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Other iPhones use built-in captions.

**I don't want a photo on a shared card.** Tap the eye button on that card to hide its photos before you share.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Contact

Open an issue: [https://github.com/lavrut/unrolled-support/issues](https://github.com/lavrut/unrolled-support/issues/new). Issues are public, so please don't include personal information, photos, or purchase receipts.
