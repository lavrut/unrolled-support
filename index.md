# Unrolled Support

Unrolled turns your camera roll into a year-in-photos recap. Everything runs on your iPhone; your photos are never uploaded.

## Common questions

**How does it work?** Tap **How it works** on the home screen for a 30-second tour. Tap the right side of a card to go forward and the left side to go back.

**Restore my purchase.** Open any locked card, tap **Unlock**, then **Restore purchase**. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Once unlocked, you'll see **Full deck unlocked** at the bottom of your cards.

**Some photos are missing from my recap.** If you chose *Limit Access*, Unrolled only sees the photos you selected. On the home screen, tap **Add more photos** to pick more, or **Allow full access** to open Settings. Photos stored in iCloud are downloaded to your iPhone as needed, so a connection helps the first time.

**There's no places or selfies card.** The places card needs photos with location info, and the selfies card uses your iPhone's Selfies album. If neither has photos from this year, that card is skipped.

**Captions look generic.** Personalized captions need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Other iPhones use built-in captions.

**Who are the characters in Roast Mode?** Each roast is delivered by one of Unrolled's plush mascots. New mascots arrive in app updates.

**I don't want a photo on a shared card.** Tap the eye button on that card to hide its photos before you share.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
