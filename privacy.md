# Unrolled Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Unrolled does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

**Your photos stay on your iPhone.** Unrolled reads your photo library only on your device to create your recap. Photos, thumbnails, and statistics are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We do not operate any servers.

**iCloud Photos.** If your photos are stored in iCloud (for example with "Optimize iPhone Storage" on), Unrolled may download small previews of them from your own iCloud to your iPhone so they can appear on your cards. This happens between your iPhone and your iCloud account; nothing is uploaded, and nothing reaches us.

**Places.** To name the cities in your recap, the app asks Apple Maps to look up a few photo locations. That request goes to Apple under Apple's privacy policy; it never reaches us.

**Captions.** On supported iPhones, captions are written by Apple's on-device language model. Nothing is sent off your device.

**Purchases.** Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment information or Apple Account.

**No tracking.** Unrolled has no ads, analytics, accounts, or third-party SDKs.

**Sharing.** Cards are only shared when you tap Share and choose where to send them.

**Children.** Unrolled does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Contact.** Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/lavrut/unrolled-support/issues. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information or photos.
